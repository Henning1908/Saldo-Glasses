SETUP GUIDE · META DISPLAY GLASSES

Your money.
In one clear glance.

Open the Saldo web app on your glasses to review your finance overview and enter transactions. Saldo starts empty, stores only what you enter, and does not connect to a bank.

Open Saldo on your glasses ↗Open the dashboard ↗

1. Add Saldo to your glasses

01

Turn on Developer Mode

In the Meta AI app, enable Developer Mode for your glasses. Meta’s Web Apps setup guide explains the current setup requirements.

02

Connect the Web App

In Meta AI, open App Settings → Apps → Web Apps → Connect Web App. Enter https://saldo-glasses.henning-orbit-calendar.workers.dev/glasses.html, save it, then open Saldo from the app grid on your glasses.

2. Pair your glasses and dashboard

01

Open Saldo on the glasses

If the device is not registered yet, Saldo displays a six-digit code. The code expires after ten minutes.

02

Choose a one-time link or Saldo-only pairing

To use the same device registration across the app family, sign in to your Meta Display Apps account, open My account, choose Saldo, and enter the code. Otherwise, enter it in the Saldo dashboard directly.

03

Start tracking your money

Add your income, expenses, budgets and balances in the dashboard. Saldo keeps its own profile; your calendar and timetable stay in their apps.

Already linked your glasses? Sign in to the same Meta Display Apps account. Saldo can recognize the device without asking for another pairing code. The app still needs its own profile and any app-specific setup.

In the Meta Display Apps family

Use Orbit Calendar for calendar events and WebUntis Glasses for your timetable. Each app has its own data and privacy details.
